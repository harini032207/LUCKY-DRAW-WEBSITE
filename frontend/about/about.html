ABOUT ENLIT

The Literary Soul of the English Department

The Club

A vibrant space where people, ideas, and creativity come together. From planning and promoting events to exploring literature, cinema, and creative expression, we create opportunities to connect, collaborate, and make things happen.

Our Vision

To build a vibrant community where creativity, connection, and collaboration inspire students to explore, express, and lead.

Our Mission

To create engaging events, experiences, and opportunities that bring students together, encourage creativity, and turn ideas into action.

Why Join ENLIT?

We’re a social networking club where you can meet new people, build meaningful connections, and be part of something beyond the classroom.

Get involved in event planning, promotions, literature, and creative activities while developing your communication, teamwork, and leadership skills.
