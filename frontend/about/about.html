ABOUT ENLIT

The Literary Soul of the English Department

The Club

ENLIT is more than just a departmental society; it is a sanctuary for those who find magic in words. We bridge the gap between classical literature and modern cinematic experiences.

Our Vision

To foster an environment where every student of the English Department can express their creativity through poetry, film, and academic discourse.

Our Mission

Organizing events that challenge the intellect and soothe the soul—from movie screenings to literary festivals.

Why Join ENLIT?

Membership offers more than just certificate points. It offers a chance to lead, to create, and to be part of a community that understands the depth of human expression.


Experience cinematic screenings, open mic nights, and exclusive departmental gatherings.
